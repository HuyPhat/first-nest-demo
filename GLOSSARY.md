# First Nest Demo

A learning and portfolio project that starts as user sign-up and sign-in and is intended to grow into an e-commerce app.

## Language

**User**:
A person who has registered with an email and password so they can sign in.
_Avoid_: Account, Member, Customer (Customer is reserved for the ordering context, not yet defined)

**Registration**:
The act of a person becoming a User by providing a unique email and a password.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, enrolment

**Login**:
The act of a User proving who they are with their email and password.
_Avoid_: Sign-in, authentication
